What is the BOI?

The Thailand Board of Investment (BOI) is the government agency that grants incentives to businesses meeting its promotion criteria, to encourage investment in Thailand. Approved applicants receive a promotion certificate that sets out the incentives and the conditions they must follow.

Incentives investors commonly look for

  • Corporate income tax exemption or reduction for a set period
  • Import duty exemption or reduction on machinery and, in some cases, raw materials
  • Permission for foreign nationals to own land for the promoted business
  • Permission to bring in foreign specialists and skilled workers, subject to conditions
  • Foreign shareholding, subject to the conditions of each activity type

Actual incentives vary by activity type, location and the conditions the BOI announces from time to time. We verify the details for you before filing.

BOI consulting: frequently asked questions

Which businesses should apply for BOI promotion?

Businesses in activity types the BOI promotes, which include manufacturing and certain services. Check your activity type and conditions before applying.

How long does a BOI application take?

It depends on the activity type, completeness of documents and the agency's process. We give an approximate timeline after an initial assessment.

Can a consultant guarantee BOI approval?

No consultant can guarantee the outcome, because approval is at the BOI's discretion. We help make your application complete, clear and aligned with the requirements.

Can foreign-owned companies apply for BOI?

Yes, subject to the conditions for each activity type. We also provide work permit and visa support for foreign executives and specialists connected to a BOI project.

How are BOI consulting fees calculated?

Fees depend on scope and activity type. We quote after an initial discussion, with no fixed rate.

What happens after the BOI promotion certificate is issued?

You must comply with the approved conditions, such as importing machinery through the proper procedure, reporting project progress and claiming incentives. We can coordinate and follow up on these steps.
